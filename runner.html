Shape Dash

How to play

  1. You are the red square. You run automatically.
  2. Jump over the black spikes, blue blocks and red domes.
  3. Grab yellow coins for bonus points.
  4. Reach 600 m to win. The game gets faster every level.

Jump with Space, ↑, or by clicking / tapping the game.
